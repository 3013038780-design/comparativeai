## Why this axis lives here

The **normative force of these frameworks is generally weak** (they are soft law or voluntary), but they are often invoked by legislation — the EU AI Act references ISO and NIST, and China's *Interim Measures on Generative AI* reference TC260 standards. Treating them as a **lightweight index** allows topic pages and rule pages to **cite them by link** when needed.

**We do not reproduce the substance of these frameworks here.** Both the OECD and UNESCO publish official Chinese translations; linking out is more accurate than copying over.
